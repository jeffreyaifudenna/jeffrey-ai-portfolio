    Daily AI News Report

    An AI task I scheduled to research what happened in AI over the past 24 hours and send me a report every morning at 8 AM, so I keep up with new models and tools.

    The plan it runs onSummarize the most important AI news from the past 24 hours. Cover new model and product launches, big company announcements, notable research, and new AI tools for image, video and website building. Give each item 2–3 sentences, a link to the source, and one line on why it matters. Keep it to the top 5–8 stories, most important first.
