Experimenting with Simple Games

November 2026 · Jeffrey

What it is

I’m not a game developer. I wanted to see how far a small idea gets when I describe it and Claude builds it. Each game below is one web page, played with a mouse, with no installs.

First try: Blocky

My first try was a single boss. A kid drew him, and I made him playable. You hold click to inhale the orange balls he throws.

Then a whole ocean

Droplet started as one file: a water droplet that inhales things, grows, and turns grumpy creatures into friends. It has no death, no timers and no way to lose.

Two 5-year-olds help design it, and their drawings are the art. Their picks decide what goes in. Their cannon boat is now a boss, and Big Green swims in a world of brick castles.

What I learned

  • Start with one small thing that works, then add to it.
  • Make each creature and level a short card, so adding one never breaks the rest.
  • Let the kids test and vote. They find problems I never would.
  • Rules written down first, like “nothing hurts the droplet”, keep later changes on track.

Where it stands

Droplet is still being built and isn’t online yet. Phones come after the computer version feels right.
