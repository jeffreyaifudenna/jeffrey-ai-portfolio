AI Job Search Pipeline

I directed Claude to run a team of 37 AI agents that found, double-checked and ranked 829 job listings for my background in under a day.

How it works

Claude split the search into segments, ran agents in parallel and wrote a Python script that scores each role 0–100 for fit, interview odds, pay, career value and commute. I set the goals, corrected my background mid-run and decided what to check and cut.

What it found

  • 829roles, 15 categories
  • 407confirmed open on the employer's site
  • 30deadlines in two weeks

Every role gets a score, AI-written notes on fit and the biggest gap. Roles split into 321 core career roles, 342 stretch roles and 166 income-now roles, with filters and the nearest deadlines at the top. Counts are from the pipeline's own dashboard, Oct 31, 2026.

How I kept it accurate

  • Rechecked 143 priority roles: 10 had closed, including my #3 pick.
  • Independent audit: a separate agent fixed 73 scores and removed 20 listings.
  • Safe browsing: hidden instructions on some job pages were ignored and flagged.

Built with

Claude (coordinating 37 agents) · Python · web search · a one-page dashboard. Employer names are left out here on purpose.
